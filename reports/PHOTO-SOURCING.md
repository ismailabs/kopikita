# Product photography review — release blocked

Reviewed on 20 October 2026 against the unchanged `data/menu-baseline.csv`. **26 of 30 products have verified, genuine, licensed photographs. Four exact variants remain unresolved.** The 30 menu records are intact; V4 is not ready to deploy.

The [photo contact sheet](photo-contact-sheet.html) links each accepted photograph to its source page and license. `data/product-photos.json` records creator, license, source and derivative paths, source-byte hashes, dimensions, alt text and match decisions. Public credits appear beside each menu item and in the footer. Adapted CC BY-SA images retain the required share-alike terms. Every accepted menu image is independent photography, not an operating KopiKita product shoot. No generated art is counted as product photography.

Wikimedia Commons API discovery and some image downloads previously returned HTTP 429; Openverse returned HTTP 403. Those responses were respected. No authentication, rate limit or access control was bypassed. Flickr’s all-rights-reserved candidates, the inaccessible Commons image, the recipe-publisher photos, and the visually mismatched Pexels photos are not integrated into the site. Raw candidate metadata and temporary inspection copies remain under ignored `artifacts/v4/photo-research/` and are excluded from the public build.

These searches record specific candidate dispositions; they do not prove that suitable free photographs exist nowhere. The current `npm run release:gate` fails only for products 02, 04, 26 and 30. The product-photo and overall visual gates remain blocked until those exact images have accessible sources and verified reuse rights. No deployment has been attempted. Completion can resume when matching, zero-cost licensed photos are available; changing the menu descriptions or buying images would require a separate change to the approved constraints.
