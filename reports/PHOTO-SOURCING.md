# Product photography review — release blocked

Reviewed on 20 October 2026. Canonical source: unchanged `data/menu-baseline.csv`. All 30 stable product IDs remain present. **23 genuine photographic depictions are integrated; seven exact variants are unresolved.** This is not a completed 30-photo collection.

The [contact sheet](photo-contact-sheet.html) has creator, individual source page and license links for every accepted image. `data/product-photos.json` records actual metadata, downloaded-byte hashes, responsive dimensions, derivative paths, alt text and review decisions. Public attribution is next to each item and in the footer. CC BY-SA derivatives retain the stated license. A photo being freely licensed does not establish its product match.

Commons API discovery and original downloads encountered HTTP 429. The documented standard cached-thumbnail path was then used at a paced rate for permitted successful downloads. Openverse returned HTTP 403. These responses were respected; no authentication or access control was bypassed. Raw discovery metadata and downloaded research copies remain local under `artifacts/v4/photo-research/`. Rejected candidates are excluded from the website build and are not silently relabelled.

The supplied `check_product_photos.py` currently fails with 49 missing/unverified field findings across exactly seven rows. The new `npm run release:gate` fails on those seven products before deployment. No gate has been weakened to accommodate them. The source checklist's execution columns were updated; its original handoff hash is therefore historical after ingestion, while the canonical menu text was checked unchanged.

This is a bounded source-verification result, not a claim that no suitable free photographs exist anywhere. Finishing requires suitable, inspectable photographs and their actual rights/provenance for these seven records. Neither generated art nor a loosely related image can close the gate.

## Follow-up source review

Targeted public catalog and reuse-page searches on 20 October 2026 surfaced additional leads but closed none of the seven rows. The exact Gula Melaka latte listing remains paid stock. Wikimedia Commons' CC0 `Rose coconut cold brew.jpg` explicitly depicts the rose flavour and was rejected. The Commons orange-tonic images establish espresso and tonic but not orange; the separate coffee-and-orange result does not establish tonic. A Commons hot-kopi candidate describes both evaporated and condensed milk, so it does not establish this menu's Kopi C. Search results for pandan kaya roll and mango vanilla-cream tart surfaced recipe publishers, not image licenses authorizing local redistribution. These findings narrow candidate dispositions; they do not establish that no suitable free photograph exists.
